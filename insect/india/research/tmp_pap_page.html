    • Arunachal is jam-packed with things to do. The hardest part of planning your holiday will be deciding which to do first! Or if it’s a relaxing holiday you’re after, hot pools, vineyards and cultural attractions will keep you entertained.

  • Arrival Formalities
    • eILP is applicable for Indian citizens only. Foreign Nationals may apply for PAP/RAP. Tourist eILP is applicable for stay up to 14 days only. Applicants for stay beyond 14 days may apply for provisional ILP. Individual eILP is not required for those below 14 years of age. They are required to be accompanied by an adult. All information furnished are subject to verification. Any wrong/misleading information may invite penal action.
    • For Domestic Tourist
      • Apply For Inner Line Permit

      • Apply For Protected Area Permit

About

India’s wildest and least explored state, Arunachal (literally the ‘Land of Dawn-lit Mountains’) rises abruptly from the Assam plains as a mass of densely forested, and impossibly steep, hills, which eventually top off as snow-capped peaks along the Tibetan border.
