I don't think you'll see Odontolabis or Hexathrius in Mumbai or in Bangalore. So I really recommend you visit some place in the Western Ghats and spend a couple of days. 5 Jul 03:16 PM
 ashwinv	
I think in general Odontolabis and Prosopocoilus are both relatively uncommon and show up (sometimes in good numbers) during a few days every monsoon. I don't think we know enough about them to predict exactly when they are likely to be most abundant. August is also very much the monsoon and you'll have a decent chance of bumping into one. I've seen them come to light but also randomly in the forest undergrowth. I would recommend carrying a white bedsheet and a UV light to set up a moth/insect screen. I've had them show up at such screens previously.

My memory of exact dates/locations are only what I've uploaded on iNaturalist. All my recent observations including the Honey Valley one have exact dates/locations.
