## 問い合わせ文テンプレ

英語:

```text
Hello, I am planning a natural history trip to South India in August and I am especially interested in observing and photographing three South Indian Odontolabis stag beetles: O. burmeisteri, O. delesserti, and O. versicolor.

I found your public observation/posts and wondered if you might be willing to share general advice about season, habitat, and whether there are local guides or estates where night macro observation can be arranged legally and respectfully. I am not asking for collecting insects or private exact coordinates; observation/photography and permission-based access are my only goals.

If paid guiding or an introduction to a suitable local naturalist is possible, I would be happy to discuss dates and fees.

Thank you very much.
```

短め:

```text
Hello, I saw your public Odontolabis observation from South India. I am visiting in August for observation/photography only, not collecting. Would you be open to sharing general advice or suggesting a local guide/estate where night macro observation can be arranged with permission?
```

## 注意点

- 野生クワガタの採集や持ち出しを連想させる語は避ける。`collect`, `specimen`, `buy`, `export` は使わない。
- `observe`, `photograph`, `night macro walk`, `with permission`, `no collecting` を前面に出す。
- exact localityをいきなり聞かず、「一般的な時期・標高・合法的に入れる宿/農園/ガイド」を聞く。
- 私有地や保護区は、必ず宿・農園・ガイド・森林局ルールを通す。
